For companies

Find the right workers, faster

SiteHired is designed to help construction companies quickly find qualified, available workers for their projects.

Instead of relying solely on traditional recruitment channels, employers can search a network of construction workers based on the specific requirements of each role.

Flat SaaS pricing, no 15–30% agency commission per hire.

AI-matched to your live project schedule, not a stale CV pile.

01

Find workers based on what matters

Specify the requirements of a role, including:

  • Trade
  • Skills
  • Experience
  • Qualifications
  • Location
  • Availability
  • Pay
  • Start date

SiteHired can then match the role with suitable workers.

02

See the full picture

Worker profiles can bring together key information including:

  • Trade and specialist skills
  • Qualifications and tickets
  • Years of experience
  • Previous projects and employers
  • Location and availability
  • Reviews and work history

Giving employers a clearer picture before making contact.

Vetting done once, trusted everywhere Right-to-work, quals and background verified once, carried across every project.

03

Find local workers

Location-based matching helps employers identify suitable workers near a project and understand how far candidates are willing to travel.

04

Build a trusted workforce

Successful jobs and employer feedback can help build a record of reliable workers.

Over time, employers can easily identify people they’ve worked with before and build a trusted network for future projects.

05

Looking ahead

As SiteHired builds recruitment and project data, our aim is to develop tools that help employers understand future labour requirements and identify potential workforce shortages earlier.
