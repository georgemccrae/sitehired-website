Privacy

How we handle your information

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This website

This is an information website. It has no accounts, and it does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Fonts and images are served from this site, so visiting it does not send your details to third parties such as Google.

Like any website, our hosting provider (Netlify) automatically records basic technical information (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) in server logs, to keep the site secure and running.

If you contact us

If you use our contact form or email us at hello@sitehired.com, we use what you give us — your name, email address, company or trade, and your message — only to reply to you and follow up on your enquiry. Contact form messages are stored by Netlify, our hosting provider, and forwarded to us by email.

We don’t sell your information or share it for marketing. We keep it only as long as we need it for that purpose.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email hello@sitehired.com and we’ll respond within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

The SiteHired platform

When the SiteHired platform launches, it will have its own privacy notice explaining how worker and company data — including right-to-work and qualification checks — is collected and protected.
